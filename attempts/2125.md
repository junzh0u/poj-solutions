# POJ 2125 — Destroying The Graph

Parked after 5 Wrong Answers on 2026-08-06. Solved by `claude-sonnet-5`; **owed one retry on the default (stronger) model** before the park is final.

## Reading of the statement

A directed graph. Bob may, for each vertex, pay `W_v^-` to delete all arcs *leaving* v ("-" move) or `W_v^+` to delete all arcs *entering* v ("+" move). Every arc must be destroyed; minimise total cost and print the move set.

An arc (u,v) is destroyed exactly when Bob plays "-" on u or "+" on v. So the task is a minimum-weight vertex cover on a bipartite graph whose left nodes are the `u-` options and right nodes the `v+` options, with one edge per arc.

## Algorithm submitted

Min-weight bipartite vertex cover via min s-t cut (König):

- `S -> L_u` with capacity `W_u^-`
- `R_v -> T` with capacity `W_v^+`
- `L_u -> R_v` with capacity infinity, one per arc

Max-flow value is the answer. The cover is recovered from residual reachability from `S`: left vertices *not* reachable are in the cover ("-" moves), right vertices reachable are in the cover ("+" moves).

## Where a retry should look

The objective value is almost certainly right — it was confirmed against an independent max-flow library at full scale. That points at **the printed move set**, which the objective checks never validated:

1. The special judge verifies the move list, not just the total. Every trial above compared the *cost*; an exhaustive brute force over subsets would agree on cost while the emitted cover differed. Check whether the recovered cover actually destroys every arc, and whether its cost equals the reported minimum — as a direct assertion, not via the flow value.
2. König recovery has a classic sign trap: the cover is {left vertices unreachable from S} + {right vertices reachable from S} in the *residual* graph. Getting this backwards still yields a valid-looking set of the right size on many instances.
3. Confirm the expected count line and ordering. Attempt #4 already tried mirroring the sample's per-vertex ordering with no change, so ordering alone is not it — but the count line itself was never varied.
4. The brute force was written from the same reading as the solution. Derive an independent one straight from the statement's own words, and have it validate *the emitted move set* rather than the cost.
