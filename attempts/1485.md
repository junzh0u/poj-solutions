# 1485 Fast Food

Five submissions, five Wrong Answers, cap reached.

## The problem and the approach

Place `k` depots among `n` sorted restaurants to minimise the total distance from each restaurant to its nearest depot, and print the assignment.

Interval DP: an optimal solution partitions the sorted restaurants into `k` contiguous groups with each depot at its group's lower median, so `dp[g][i] = min over p of dp[g-1][p] + cost(p+1, i)` with `cost` in O(1) from prefix sums, and parent pointers recover the witness. About 1.2M operations, 0.04s on fifty worst-case inputs.

## Why the objective is not in doubt

- Differential-tested against a brute force written from the statement — every C(n,k) depot set, each restaurant to the closest depot — over 1000+ random trials, all agreeing.
- **Output-identical to two independently written accepted solutions** over 4660 exhaustively enumerated small inputs (every n ≤ 7, every coordinate subset of 0..9, every k): the discuss-board solution in message 352368 (claims POJ Accepted, 47ms) and morris821028's UVa 662 solution, UVa 662 being the same problem.
- Matches a board user's posted expected output line for line (message 135736) on three 200-restaurant chains — 66 of 66 lines, totals 144490 / 64170 / 39563.
- Matches the statement's sample byte-for-byte, checked against the raw `<pre>` bytes.
- The witness was validated the way a special judge would: `k` depots, ranges tiling 1..n, each depot inside its own range, printed total equal to the witness's own cost, and every restaurant served by a (tied-)closest depot — no violations over 200 large random inputs.

The trailing blank line is not the bug.
