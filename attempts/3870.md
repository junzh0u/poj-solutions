## Statement and algorithm

The input is one directed acyclic graph with 2 through 100 vertices, at most one edge per ordered pair, and no isolated vertices. Every edge must appear in at least one printed downward route; repeated inspections are expressly allowed. Minimize the number of routes and print their vertex sequences. The limit is 1000MS and 65536K. The discussion board was empty.

Initially regard every edge as a separate route. Its tail contributes one route start and its head one route end. Build a network from a new source to each vertex with capacity equal to its indegree, from each vertex to a new sink with capacity equal to its outdegree, and along every original arc with capacity 10000 (larger than the maximum 4950 original edges). A maximum flow joins as many ends to starts as possible, optionally following additional downward slopes. The answer is the edge count minus this maximum flow. Reverse residual capacities permit revising earlier joins. Set each original slope's multiplicity to one plus its net extra flow, and decompose into paths from the remaining starts to the remaining ends. The minimum-flow formulation proves optimality; acyclicity ensures decomposition terminates.
