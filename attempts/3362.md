# 3362 — 20-Four

The statement describes independent groups on the 4 by 4 grid of cities with coordinates 1 through 4. Every move takes 20 minutes. Initially every group faces decreasing y and has no previous city. Input specifies the first requested operation, F or L; the protocol then alternates. A forward move continues the actual previous displacement, while left rotates (dx,dy) to (dy,-dx). A missing forward neighbor is replaced by left; a missing left neighbor is replaced by backtracking to the previous city, or staying if no previous city exists. The objective appears to be the earliest simultaneous city at times 8:00 plus 20-minute increments, through 24 elapsed hours, including the initial positions.

The figure was downloaded and inspected. Its illustrated trajectory begins (4,3) with L, then (3,3), (2,3), (2,4), (3,4), consistent with the grid interpretation and rotation above. The sample groups (2,3,F) and (3,4,F) first meet at (1,3) after three moves, yielding exactly `Case 1: 1, 3, 900`.

Message 147021 confirms the clock starts at 8:00. Message 77801 observes trajectories eventually travel around the perimeter, without an authoritative answer. Message 77008 asks how midnight should be formatted and has no body or replies. No official contest data or reference implementation was found by a brief search for Manila 2006 and the title.
