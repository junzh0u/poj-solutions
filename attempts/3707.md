# 3707 — High-Dimensional Vector Correspondence

The special-judge gate was explicitly lifted for this run. The preceding gated note recorded no attempted solutions or submissions. This run used gpt-6-astra and made three submissions, all Wrong Answer; it stopped without a known failing case rather than spending the remaining two submissions on arbitrary tolerance or orientation changes.

The input is one data set: n vectors of dimension m for P, followed by n vectors for Q, with n,m at most 512. Coordinates are floating point, with no stated magnitude bound. The input rows describe column vectors. The output is m followed by an m by m orthogonal matrix H satisfying H p_i = q_i, or an asterisk if impossible. Reflections are allowed, so determinant minus one is valid. The statement's subsidiary vector formula mistakenly repeats row 1; its input matrix and the discussion establish that every corresponding row is constrained.

The approach constructs complete orthogonal factors X and Y such that XP and YQ are the same positive-diagonal row-echelon form, interpreting P and Q as m by n matrices. At step k choose the remaining column of P with maximum squared residual norm below row k; swap that column into position k in both matrices. Apply an independent orthogonal elimination to P and Q, and accumulate its left multiplication into X and Y, initially identities. Positive diagonal signs make the resulting forms canonical. Their equality is equivalent to existence of the required orthogonal map, including rank-deficient inputs. Output H = transpose(Y) X. Completing X and Y as square orthogonal matrices automatically provides a valid mapping on unconstrained dimensions.
