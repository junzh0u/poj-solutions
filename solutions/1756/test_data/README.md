The 26 input and answer pairs come from https://neerc.ifmo.ru/archive/2000/tests.rar, linked by the NEERC 2000 archive as sample tests and checkers (not official contest-result evidence). The original files are domino.01 through domino.26 and their corresponding a-suffixed answer files. Output is a witness: compare the first integer for optimal cost, then validate the declared piece count, digit range, sum, connectedness, and at most two odd-degree vertices. Equivalent optimal sets may differ from these answer files.
