The two samples and 35 further official NEERC 2005 Guards cases come from https://neerc.ifmo.ru/archive/2005/problems.rar (guards/tests/01 through 37). Each input is a separate invocation. Outputs are official optimal witnesses; other feasible witnesses with the same total are valid. All 37 were checked for minimum total against the jury answers and simulated over the full 420-day combined cycle.
