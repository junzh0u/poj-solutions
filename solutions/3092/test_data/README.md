The primary files preserve the POJ statement sample. The official files are the 53 original Greater New York 2006 problem H judge cases, downloaded from https://gny.na.icpc.global/year2006/h.in and https://gny.na.icpc.global/year2006/h.out. Outputs are witnesses: alternative sums and term orders are valid. Both published output sets and the accepted program outputs were checked for exact sums, valid exponents, term counts, and pairwise non-divisibility.
