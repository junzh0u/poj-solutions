Official NEERC 2010 problem D tests, downloaded from https://neerc.ifmo.ru/archive/2010/neerc-2010-tests.rar. All 50 input/answer pairs are preserved; test 01 is the primary sample and tests 02 through 50 retain their original numbers. The independent verifier reproduced the published checker's volume and point-containment conditions and passed every pair. Judge answers may have more decimal places, but the accepted POJ source prints exactly three: changing only its format from ten places to three changed Wrong Answer to Accepted.
